Where we start

Discovery Bible Study, a first step. From there, we go deeper.

1 · Look back
  • What are you thankful for?
  • What's been hard this week?
  • How did you obey and share last week?
2 · Look up
  • Read the passage aloud, then retell it in your own words.
  • What does this teach us about God?
  • What does this teach us about people?
3 · Look forward
  • How will I obey this?
  • Who will I share it with this week?
