Cypress College

Raising up healthy disciples who look like Christ and share Jesus with others.

We take our picture of a healthy disciple from the persecuted church: people whose faith costs them something, who know the Word, who obey it, and who can't help but tell others about Jesus.

What a healthy disciple looks like

  1. 1

    Looks like Christ

    Growing in character, holiness, and love.

    Romans 8:29 · Galatians 5:22–23
  2. 2

    Abides in the Word

    Reads, obeys, and shares Scripture.

    John 8:31–32 · James 1:22
  3. 3

    Prays

    Depends on God, not on programs.

    Luke 18:1 · 1 Thessalonians 5:17
  4. 4

    Shares Jesus

    Carries a burden for the lost on campus and beyond.

    Romans 10:14 · Luke 19:10
  5. 5

    Lives in healthy community

    Plugged into a local body of believers for accountability, worship, and care.

    Hebrews 10:24–25 · Acts 2:42–47
  6. 6

    Makes disciples

    Passes on what they've received to others.

    2 Timothy 2:2 · Matthew 28:19–20
  7. 7

    Stands firm when it costs

    Holds to God's Word against worldly pressure and influence, and endures hardship with joy.

    Romans 12:2 · 2 Timothy 3:12 · 1 Peter 4:12–14

Our focus

Lostness

Most students at Cypress College have never been invited to read the Bible with someone. We exist for them.

Multiplying disciples

We want to see more and more disciples made. We raise up student leaders who reach their friends, help them learn and obey God's Word, and train them to do the same for others.

Rooted and standing firm

Students hear many voices about identity, truth, and freedom. We help them test every idea against Scripture, stay rooted in Christ, and find real freedom in Him.

Colossians 2:6–8 · John 8:31–32, 36

Discovery Bible Study

Where we start: a way for anyone to begin reading and obeying the Bible with friends. As disciples grow, we help them go deeper into the whole counsel of God.

1 · Look back
  • What are you thankful for?
  • What's been hard this week?
  • How did you obey and share last week?
2 · Look up
  • Read the passage aloud, then retell it in your own words.
  • What does this teach us about God?
  • What does this teach us about people?
3 · Look forward
  • How will I obey this?
  • Who will I share it with this week?
