# Cypress College — Campus Vision (Draft)

> Working draft. Section 1–4 are public-facing and can appear in the app.
> Section 5 is for leaders only and is **not** shown in the app.

---

## 1. Vision

**To raise up healthy disciples who look like Christ and share Jesus with others.**

Healthy disciples reflect the character and pursuit of the persecuted
church: faithful when it costs, humble, courageous, loving even toward those
who oppose them, and joyful under pressure (Acts 5:41; Heb 10:32–34).

## 2. What a Healthy Disciple Looks Like

A healthy disciple:

1. **Looks like Christ** — growing in character, holiness, and love (Rom 8:29; Gal 5:22–23).
2. **Abides in the Word** — reads, obeys, and shares Scripture (John 8:31–32; James 1:22).
3. **Prays** — depends on God, not on programs (Luke 18:1; 1 Thess 5:17).
4. **Shares Jesus** — carries a burden for the lost on campus and beyond (Rom 10:14; Luke 19:10).
5. **Lives in healthy community** — plugged into a local body of believers for
   accountability, worship, and care (Heb 10:24–25; Acts 2:42–47).
   *We don't prescribe the form — house church or church building — only that
   it is healthy, Christ-centered, and faithful to Scripture.*
6. **Makes disciples** — passes on what they've received (2 Tim 2:2; Matt 28:19–20).
7. **Stands firm when it costs** — holds to God's Word against worldly pressure and
   influence, and endures hardship with joy (Rom 12:2; 2 Tim 3:12; 1 Pet 4:12–14).

## 3. Focus

- **Lostness.** Most students at Cypress College have never been invited to read
  the Bible with someone. We exist for them.
- **Multiplying disciples.** We want to see more and more disciples made. We
  raise up student leaders who reach their friends, help them learn and obey
  God's Word, and train them to do the same for others (2 Tim 2:2).
- **Rooted and standing firm.** Students hear many voices about identity,
  truth, and freedom. We help them test every idea against Scripture, stay
  rooted in Christ, and find real freedom in Him (Col 2:6–8; John 8:31–32, 36).

## 4. Starting Point: Discovery Bible Study (DBS)

Discovery Bible Study is the foundation we start with. It gets anyone reading
and obeying God's Word right away, and it's a starting place, not a ceiling:
as disciples mature, we help them go deeper into the whole counsel of God
(Acts 20:27; Heb 5:12–14).

1. **Look back** — What are you thankful for? What's been hard? How did you
   obey and share last week?
2. **Look up** — Read the passage aloud. Retell it in your own words.
   - What does this teach us about God?
   - What does this teach us about people?
3. **Look forward** — How will I obey this? Who will I share it with this week?

Starting location: **Cypress College**.

## 5. Leader Convictions & Discernment *(internal — not displayed in the app)*

Leaders are equipped to lead people into the freedom of Christ (John 8:36;
Gal 5:1) and to hold to Scripture without compromise, while speaking the truth
in love (Eph 4:15) and always being ready to give a gentle, respectful answer
(1 Pet 3:15). We disagree with ideas, never dehumanize people — everyone we
meet is someone Jesus came to save.

Leaders should be equipped to address, from Scripture:

- **Cults and false teaching** — knowing the essentials (the Trinity, the deity
  and bodily resurrection of Christ, salvation by grace through faith, the
  sufficiency of Scripture) and recognizing groups that deny them
  (Gal 1:6–9; 1 John 4:1–3; 2 Pet 2:1).
- **Sexuality and identity** — a biblical view of gender and sexuality
  (Gen 1:27; Gen 2:24; Matt 19:4–6; 1 Cor 6:9–11), and an identity rooted in
  Christ rather than desire or self-definition.
- **Manhood and womanhood** — the equal worth of men and women as image-bearers
  and their distinct, complementary design (Gen 1:27; Eph 5:22–33), as opposed
  to ideologies that set the sexes against each other.
- **Race and justice** — unity and equal dignity in Christ across every ethnicity
  (Gal 3:28; Eph 2:14–16; Rev 7:9), with sin, repentance, and reconciliation
  understood biblically rather than through frameworks that assign identity,
  guilt, or worth by group.
- **Worldly ideologies in general** — testing every philosophy against Christ
  (Col 2:8; Rom 12:2; 2 Cor 10:5).

**How we display this:** The app and public materials lead with Jesus, the
Word, and invitation. Convictions are taught in leader training, one-on-one
discipleship, and as passages come up naturally in DBS — not as public
messaging. Keep a written statement of faith available for anyone who asks.
